Frequently asked questions

Some quick answers about getting started and working together.

What can WIZARDZ help with?

Our services include SEO, PPC advertising, social media marketing, email marketing, and content creation. We can discuss which channels best fit your goals.

How do we get started?

Start with a conversation about your business, audience, and goals. From there, we can identify priorities and discuss a suitable scope of work.

How soon will I see results?

Timing varies by channel, competition, budget, and starting point. Paid campaigns can provide earlier feedback, while organic growth often takes longer. No specific result or timeline can be guaranteed.

How do you measure campaign performance?

Measurement should follow your goals. We review relevant performance signals and use what we learn to guide future campaign improvements.

Do I need to use every service?

No. The right mix depends on your audience and objectives. We can focus on the channels that make sense for your business.
